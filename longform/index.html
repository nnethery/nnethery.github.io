Longform

Longform is an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that helps you write a first draft of a short story, novella or novel, one session at a time.

You choose your writing days and a start date. Longform builds a plan of writing sessions on those days, and the plan adapts when you skip or move a session, with no catch-up.
