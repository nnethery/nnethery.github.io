What we collect and why

Our plain position:

  • Usage analytics are on when you start. You can turn them off in Profile at any time.
  • We use your data, including what you write, to run Longform and to make it better.
  • We never sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your account

You need an account to use Longform. You can sign up with an email address and password, with Sign in with Apple, or with Google.

  • Email and password. We store your email address and a hashed form of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Sign in with Apple. We ask Apple only for your email address, not your name. If you choose "Hide My Email", we receive an Apple relay address instead of your real one. We also store the identifier that Apple gives us for your account.
  • Google. We receive your email address and the identifier that Google gives us for your account.

Each account has a random account ID. Our logs and analytics name you by that ID, not by your email address. We use your email address to sign you in, to keep one account for each email address, and to reply to you when you ask us to.

We do not send marketing email.

Your writing and attachments

Longform stores what you write so that it is safe and so that it appears on each of your devices. This includes:

  • drafts, closing lines and ideas;
  • your plan: the form you chose, your writing days, your dates and session lengths, the sessions you finish, skip or move, and your answers to "How did the time feel?";
  • files you attach: photos of handwritten pages, pictures, PDFs, and text or Markdown files, from the app, the camera, your photo library, the Files app or the share sheet;
  • the word estimate for handwritten pages (the number only; see What stays on your device).

The app saves your writing on your device first. It then sends drafts, closing lines and ideas to our server in the background. Our server keeps earlier versions of each item, so that a sync mistake cannot lose your work.

We may read and analyse your writing to improve Longform, for example to understand how writers use a session or why a plan does not fit. We do not publish your writing, and we do not show it to anyone else. Longform does not send your writing to outside AI companies today. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Usage analytics

When analytics are on, the app records what you do in the app as short events. Examples: you opened a screen, started or finished a session, opened an exercise, saved an idea, or changed a setting. An event holds counts, durations, identifiers and choices from fixed lists. It never holds the text you write. Word and character counts are recorded as numbers or ranges, not as text.

Each event also carries details about its setting: your account ID, a random device ID, an app session ID, the app version and build, the device model, the system version, your language setting, your time zone offset, the network type (Wi-Fi, cellular or none), and the plan week you are in. The device ID is a random number made when you install the app. It is not Apple's advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The app records a few events before you sign in, such as the first launch and the sign-up screens. Those events carry the device ID but no account ID.

We use analytics to learn where new writers get stuck, which features help, and whether plans fit real lives.

To turn analytics off, open Profile and turn off "Share usage data to improve Longform". The app then stops recording events and deletes the events still waiting on your device.

Our server also records a few events that it sees directly: when an account is created, when your schedule is recalculated, when a new plan week starts, and which feature settings it applied. It keeps a small analytics profile for each account: the date you started, the form of your plan, your time zone offset and your language. Today the Profile switch controls what the app sends. It does not stop these server records.

Diagnostics

The app and our server keep technical logs so that we can find and fix problems. A log record holds a fixed message, such as "upload failed", and numbers such as a duration, a byte count or an error code. It never holds your writing. The app sends these logs to our server even when analytics are off, because we need them to keep the service working. Our server also logs each request it answers: the route, the result, the time it took, and your account ID.

Feedback

When you send feedback from the app, we receive the kind of feedback you chose, your message, any screenshots you add (up to three), the screen you came from, and whether we may email you about it.

If you turn on "Include app details to help us find the problem", we also receive the app version and build, the device model and system version, your language and time zone, the versions of the plan and text the app holds, and the last 50 short technical notes that the app kept. App details never include your drafts, closing lines, ideas or exercise text. The text you type into the feedback message is, of course, included.

If you turn on "You can email me about this", we may look up your account email address and write to you about that feedback.

Device and app information

Each time the app connects, it tells our server the app version and build, the platform (iPhone or iPad, or Apple Watch), the system version, your time zone, your language and region setting, and the local date. We keep the latest of these for each of your devices. We use them to build your schedule in the right time zone, to show text in your language, and to tell you when an update is needed.

Our server sees the IP address of your connection, because the internet needs it to send replies. Our service does not store your IP address in its logs or analytics.

Membership and purchases

A Longform membership is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card number or your Apple payment details. Apple sends us signed information about the transaction: the product, the dates, whether it renews, whether it came from an offer code, and whether it was refunded or revoked. We link that information to your Longform account so that the app knows you are a member. If we give you a membership directly, we record that on your account.

App permissions

The app asks your permission before it uses these features. You can say no, and the rest of the app still works.

  • Camera, to photograph handwritten pages and keep them with a session.
  • Notifications, for one optional reminder on session days. The reminder is scheduled on your device. Our server does not send it.
  • Photos, only for the pictures you choose. The system picker shows you your library. The app receives only the items you select.

What stays on your device

Some things never leave your device:

  • The text of handwritten pages. When you photograph handwritten pages, the app reads them on your device with Apple's built-in text recognition to estimate a word count. The recognised text stays on your device. Only the photos (as attachments) and the estimated number go to our server. You can turn this off in Profile with "Estimate words from handwritten pages".
  • Exercise text. What you type inside an exercise stays on your device unless you save a result as an idea.
  • Your settings, such as reminders, haptics, tips and the analytics switch.
  • Apple Watch. The watch app talks only to your iPhone, never to our server. A note you take on the watch reaches us only after your iPhone saves it as an idea.
  • Shares. When you share pages with a reader, the app opens Apple's share sheet. You choose what to send and to whom. We do not see it or send it.

Signing out does not erase your writing from your device. "Erase everything on this iPhone" in Profile removes drafts, closing lines, ideas, attachments and the saved plan from that device only. It does not delete your account or the copies on our server.

Where your data is stored

Our server and database are in Germany. Attachments, feedback screenshots, and the files that hold our logs and analytics are stored with Backblaze in the United States. Longform is run from the United States.

So if you live outside the United States, your data will go to and be stored in Germany and the United States. If you are in the EU or the UK, transfers to the United States rely on the safeguards that our providers offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where a provider takes part in it, and on the need to transfer data to provide the service you asked for.

What happens when you delete things

  • A draft, closing line or idea. The app removes it from your device and marks it deleted on our server. The server keeps the earlier versions until your account is deleted.
  • An attachment. We delete its record and its file. Backblaze keeps a hidden copy of the file for 30 days and then removes it for good.
  • A plan. "Delete this plan" removes the plan, its schedule, its session records and its attachments. Your drafts, closing lines and ideas stay.
  • Your account. The app does not have a button to delete your account yet. Until it does, email noah@nethery.dev from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. Within [30] days we will delete your account, your writing and all earlier versions of it, your plans, attachments, feedback, and device records. Usage events and logs carry only your random account ID; once your account is gone, nothing links that ID to you, and those records then age out on the schedule above. Copies in database backups go when those backups are replaced, and deleted attachment files go after 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple account settings.

Your rights

We give these rights to everyone, wherever you live:

  • Access. You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Export. In Profile, "Export my data" saves your drafts, closing lines, ideas, attachment records and settings from your device as one file. For a copy of what our server holds, email us.
  • Correction. You can ask us to correct data that is wrong.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete your data or your account (see above).
  • Objection and restriction. You can turn analytics off at any time in Profile. You can also ask us to stop or limit other uses of your data.
  • Portability. Your export is a standard JSON file that other software can read.
  • No discrimination. We will not treat you worse for using these rights.

To use a right, email noah@nethery.dev. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, normally by writing from the email address on the account. We will answer within one month. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and how to appeal. If someone acts for you, we need your signed permission.

If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Legal bases (EU and UK)

If the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, we rely on these legal bases:

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract): your account, your writing and attachments, sync, your plan and schedule, membership status, and device records.
  • Our legitimate interests: usage analytics, diagnostics, reading writing and feedback to improve Longform, and keeping the service secure. We think these uses are expected for an app like this and limited in their effect on you. You can object at any time, and you can turn analytics off in Profile.
  • Your consent: camera, photos and notifications (through the system's permission prompts), and email replies about feedback. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: records that tax law or a court requires us to keep.

Where the law of your country requires consent for analytics, the app will ask for it before it records analytics.

Longform does not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. The plan engine adapts your schedule, and you can always change it.

  • You have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and the right not to be treated worse for using these rights. To use them, email noah@nethery.dev.

How we secure your data

  • All data between the app and our server travels over HTTPS.
  • Attachments are private. The app uploads and downloads them with links that expire, and Backblaze encrypts the stored files.
  • Our database and sign-in service have no connection to the internet. Only our server can reach them.
  • The app stores your sign-in session in the iOS Keychain.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise that your data will never be accessed without permission. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Longform is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under the age at which your country lets you agree to data processing yourself (up to 16 in parts of the EU), you need a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy when the law or our practices change. When we make a significant change, we will change the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
